About

I'm a journalism student in the College of Media and Communication at Texas Tech University and a sports reporter for The Daily Toreador, Texas Tech's independent student newspaper. I cover women's soccer, women's basketball, track and field, cross country and golf, writing previews, recaps and features on deadline.

Before I got to Lubbock, I built my own newsroom. I founded Track & XC News to cover running at the high school, collegiate and professional levels, and Track & XC Portal, which covers NCAA recruiting, commitments and transfers and introduced the first-ever star rating system for high school track and field athletes.

I ran cross country myself at Wylie High School, where our team won four straight district titles and qualified for state twice. That time on the course shapes how I report on the sport today: from the infield at the USATF Outdoor Championships to the finish line at Nike Cross Regionals South.
